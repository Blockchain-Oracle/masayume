## Visual direction

Use Masayume black, warm white and burnt orange, the exact mark, compact headlines and generous spacing. The banner's ribbon through three frames suggests connected markets, games and agents. It is decorative art, not a price prediction.

Service replies should be useful receipts. Keep the actual status first and the transaction link in adjacent text. The small paper receipt supplies personality. Memes fit occasional editorial posts better than extra promotional replies under every mention.
